## Full description (4000 char max)

```
You are a courier on antigrav rollers, running the length of a pastel orbital
city. Swipe to switch lane, up to jump, down to slide. That is the whole
control scheme, and you will have it in three seconds.

Your CHARGE drains constantly. RELAY gates refill it, CELLS extend it, and
crashing never kills you outright — it costs charge and speed, which costs you
more charge. You die when the bar empties, never on impact. Someone who has
never played a runner will get somewhere on their first try. Getting far is
another matter.

ELEVEN ZONES, ELEVEN SETS OF RULES

Every zone changes what you are actually doing, not just what it looks like.

• THE RING — three lanes, pastel avenues, the grammar of the game
• THE SHORE — no road at all, just open sea. Clear the swells in the air
• THE SUGAR FLATS — real hills. You bleed speed climbing and get it back falling
• THE MARKET — grind rails to ride above the barriers
• THE GREENHOUSE — hedges block every lane. Only a bloom pad clears them
• THE DOCKS — the catwalk runs out. Low gravity, long jumps, real gaps
• THE ARCADE — you are the ball. Chain the bumpers or the bar dies
• THE HEIGHTS — a bridge with panels missing and no railings
• THE VAULT — you fly. Up and down pick an altitude, not a jump
• THE BOTTLING PLANT — belt lanes. Mint carries you, red syrup drags you
• THE CORE — everything at once, and faster

NO POWER-UPS, NO UPGRADES, NO WAITING

There is nothing to buy and nothing to unlock with money. You do not get
stronger between runs — you get better. Clean play pays charge back, so the
difference between a short run and a long one is entirely you.

Zones unlock by finishing the previous one, which cannot be ground out.

MADE FOR A PHONE

One thumb. Portrait. Works fully offline. No account, no ads, no tracking,
nothing collected. Twelve original tracks.
```

## Data safety form

- Does your app collect or share user data? **No**
- Is data encrypted in transit? Not applicable, nothing is transmitted
- Can users request deletion? Not applicable, nothing is stored off device

Local high scores live in the app's own storage and are removed when the app
is uninstalled. That is not "collection" under Play's definition.
